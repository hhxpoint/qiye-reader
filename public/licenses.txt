CONTENT AND PRIVACY
The three starter texts are original QIYE teaching examples, not published papers or measured experiments. There are no prewritten translation outputs.
User documents and reading notes are processed and stored in the browser. Application and runtime assets are fetched from this website. Fixed-revision public ONNX weights are fetched from hf-mirror.com, with Hugging Face as an alternate source. Neither receives document text. Download availability depends on these external services. Optional built-in browser AI downloads are managed by the browser vendor. No cloud language-model endpoint is used.
Local storage may be deleted or evicted. Use the complete backup feature. This first version has no OCR, automatic cloud synchronization, or guarantee of support for every language, device or PDF layout.
